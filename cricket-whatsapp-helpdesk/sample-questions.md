# Sample parent questions

Use these to test the help desk (with `test-mock.sh` or real WhatsApp). Player names match the sample squads, so every question has a real answer in the sheet. Run the **Tournament Simulator** up to the date shown first, so the results exist.

## Before the tournament (no simulation needed)
1. When does the tournament start and when is the final?
2. Which group is Royal Strikers in, and who else is in that group?
3. When and where is Thunder Kings' first match?
4. My son plays for Lion Hearts. What time should he reach the ground on 17 Oct?
5. Which team is Kabir A6 in and when is his first game?
6. How many overs is each match?
7. How do teams qualify for the semi-finals?
8. Is there any match on 21 Oct?

## During the league stage (simulate up to 18, 19 or 20 Oct)
9. Who won yesterday's matches?
10. What was the score in Royal Strikers vs Thunder Kings?
11. Show me the points table.
12. Which teams are in the top two of Group B right now?
13. How many runs has Aarav A1 scored so far?
14. How many wickets has Neel B11 taken in the tournament?
15. Who is the top run scorer so far?
16. Who has taken the most wickets?
17. Who was Player of the Match in match 4?
18. What is Golden Titans' net run rate?
19. When is Eagle Eleven's next match?
20. Can Blue Warriors still qualify for the semi-final?

## Knockouts (simulate up to 22 or 23 Oct)
21. Who is playing in the semi-finals?
22. Who won the first semi-final and by how much?
23. Which two teams are in the final, and what time does it start?
24. Who won the tournament?
25. Who was Player of the Match in the final?
26. Give me the final stats for Dev G1 for the whole tournament.

## Should be politely declined (not in the sheet)
27. What is the prize money?
28. Can my daughter join a team now?
29. What's the weather forecast for Saturday?
30. What is the academy's fee for next term?
